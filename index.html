About

I'm an engineer by training. Early on, I helped pioneer music streaming at Beats Music and carried that work into Apple Music. From there I helped pioneer direct-to-consumer commerce at Dollar Shave Club, and I've since been CTO three times: at Dollar Shave Club, at Authentic Brands Group, and as Global Enterprise CTO at Shopify, where I worked with some of the biggest brands in commerce.

My years at Apple and Beats, at the intersection of product and culture, still shape how I build: design first.

I joined Salesforce in 2024 as an Architect in the Office of the CEO, working with Fortune 500 customers across industries. Today I serve as Chief Business Officer and Chief of Staff to Marc Benioff, leading the Office of the CEO and helping shape company-wide priorities and Salesforce's approach to trusted AI and data.

Outside of work, I'm a proud husband and father of two boys and a baby girl.
